Questions

Good to know

Why does macOS say Isle can’t be opened?

The first time only, right-click Isle in Applications and choose Open. After that it opens normally and starts on its own when you log in.

Will it tell me when Xcode finishes a build?

Not by itself. macOS doesn’t let one app see another’s notifications, so Isle can’t watch Xcode, Slack or Mail. It shows what you send it: add the open "isle://notify?…" line from the Playground to a build phase, script, Shortcut or git hook.

Which apps does the music player work with?

With Xcode or the Command Line Tools installed, any app that shows in Now Playing: Spotify, Music, Podcasts, browsers and more. Without them, Isle falls back to Music, Spotify, Safari and Chrome, and the browsers need “Allow JavaScript from Apple Events” turned on.

How do I update?

Download the new version and open it. Isle offers to replace the old copy in Applications with one click, keeps your settings, and makes sure only one Isle is running. macOS may ask for Calendar once more; just click Allow.

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On an external display or a Mac without a notch, Isle draws one of the same size, and everything behaves the same.

Can it show Messages or Slack notifications?

No. macOS doesn’t let any app read another app’s notifications. Isle shows its own alerts and anything your Shortcuts send to it.
